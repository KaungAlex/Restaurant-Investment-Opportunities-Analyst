# 🍽️ Restaurant Investment Opportunities Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Restaurant Investment Opportunities Dashboard developed in Power BI to support data-driven restaurant expansion and investment decisions.

The dashboard analyzes consumer demographics, spending behavior, cuisine preferences, and geographic distribution to identify high-potential markets and target customer segments for new restaurant investments.

Through advanced analytics and interactive visualizations, the solution provides actionable insights that help investors, business owners, and decision-makers evaluate market opportunities and optimize investment strategies.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to identify the most attractive market opportunities for restaurant investment by analyzing:

- Consumer demographics
- Income distribution
- Occupation profiles
- Cuisine preferences
- Smoking behavior
- Geographic concentration of customers

The dashboard enables stakeholders to make informed decisions regarding restaurant location selection, customer targeting, and menu strategy.

## 🔍 Executive Summary

Based on the analysis:

- **San Luis Potosi** represents the most attractive investment location, accounting for approximately **61% of the consumer market**.
- **Medium-income consumers** represent the largest customer segment at **37.4%**.
- **Students** constitute the most significant occupational group within the target market.
- The majority of consumers are **non-smokers (80.15%)**.
- **Mexican cuisine** is the most preferred cuisine category among consumers.

These findings suggest that a restaurant targeting medium-income students in San Luis Potosi would have the highest potential for market success.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

### Location Recommendation

San Luis Potosi demonstrates the strongest investment potential due to its high consumer concentration and favorable demographic profile.

### Target Market

The ideal target audience consists of:

- Students
- Medium-income consumers
- Non-smokers

### Cuisine Strategy

Mexican cuisine represents the strongest market opportunity, followed by Cafeteria and Seafood concepts.

### Market Expansion Potential

Consumer distribution analysis highlights several secondary markets that may support future expansion initiatives.
